What should I listen for?
  • Letter names: the spoken name must match the displayed name; check similar consonants.
  • Syllables: check the written vowel, short versus long sound, tanween, sukun, glides and doubled consonants. Do not approve a letter name in place of a syllable.
  • Words: check every consonant, vowel and written ending. These are isolated teaching exercises, not connected recitation.
  • Clip quality: the beginning and ending must be intact, with no neighbouring item, clicks or distracting noise. The voice should sound calm and clear.
  • Joined name prompts: check the name order and gaps. These reuse existing letter-name clips; they do not demonstrate blended reading or qualified recitation.

“Correct” becomes available after the whole item has played. Use “Needs fixing” for the wrong sound or a bad cut; add what you heard or what should change. “Unsure” is fine. Reviews are tied to the exact audio: a changed clip needs another listen.
