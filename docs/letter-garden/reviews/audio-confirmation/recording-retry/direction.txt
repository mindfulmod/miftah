Speak in Arabic with a warm, gentle teaching voice for children aged 4–6. Read each non-empty line exactly once, in order, with one full second of silence between lines. These are vowelled syllables, not letter names or a connected sentence. Preserve the written consonant and the distinction between a short vowel and a long vowel. Finish each syllable cleanly before pausing. Do not add a final consonant, extra vowel, label, number, explanation or commentary. Do not omit, combine or repeat items.
