# Watching a child play — a 15-minute guide (2026-10-09)

The only real test of "nothing should feel discouraging" is a child. This is
how to run one with no setup.

## Before
- Open the game with `?playtest=clear` added to the address once (empties the
  log), then normally with `?playtest`. Logging stays on this device only.
- Use the child's own iPad or phone, sound on, in their usual spot.
- Say as little as possible. If they ask, "what do you think?" is enough.

## While they play — watch, don't help
Write down the time and the game when you see any of these:
- **A pause longer than ~5s** with no touch (are they lost, or thinking?).
- **The same wrong thing tapped twice** — did the helping hand appear, and
  did they follow it?
- **A sigh, a frown, "I can't", looking away, handing the device back.**
- **Tapping during a wait** (after a right answer, during "look" time) —
  did it feel answered?
- **Smiles and "again!"** — just as important; note what caused them.
- **Leaving a game early** (Home) — what happened right before?

## Worth a special look (late v58 changes)
Each of these was a judgement call made without a child. Your notes settle them:
- **Little Sprout (age 2–3):** games are now 3 rounds, not 4. Does a toddler
  still finish happy, or want more? Confetti now shows on their reward
  screens. Excited, or too much? (A grown-up's own Gentle mode keeps it off.)
- **The chapter party** now shows the child's *best* game's stars, not the
  average. Does a child who struggled early light up at the end?
- **The pet's faces** were softened. On a miss it gives a lopsided "hmm?"
  smile, and while they choose it looks up with interest. Does the child
  ever read the pet as cross or sad?
- **Tapping the thing they just got right** (a found bubble, a finished
  letter, a fed friend) now carries on to the next round. Do they discover
  this, or hunt for the arrow?
- **Dot Garden:** after three wrong checks the dots are placed for them.
  Relief, or does it feel like being done-for? Does the glowing arrow at the
  end of exploring get noticed?

### Added 2026-10-10 (later v58 judgement calls)
- **Colour a Friend:** colouring the letter alone now brings the "hang it"
  button. Does the child keep colouring for the fun of it, or hang at once
  and lose interest? Either is fine — note which.
- **Muted by accident:** if sound goes off, tapping "hear it again" makes
  the speaker bounce and the hand point at it. Does the child (or you)
  notice and turn it back on?
- **Wind story:** the pet now watches the gust instead of an empty page. Does
  the "friends blown away" moment ever upset the child, or is it a fun
  mystery? Watch their face on the second page.
- **Letter Balloons / Fishing:** letters are bigger and nothing overlaps on
  phones. Does a toddler pop/catch the one they meant to?
- **Sideways phone:** Busy Market, Letter Delivery and Letter Studio were
  re-laid out for a sideways phone. If the child holds it sideways, does
  anything feel cramped or hard to reach?

- **A little sprout's toy screen:** all nine toys now fit one screen (smaller
  tiles on phones). Does the toddler find and try the ones at the bottom
  (sand, colouring), or only ever tap the first row?

## After
- Open the game with `?playtest=report`. The table lists each game, hardest
  first: starts, finishes, games left early, right answers, comebacks (right
  after a miss), misses, helping hands, idle help, and hard-patch easing.
- A game with many misses, hands *and* early exits is the one to look at
  first. Many comebacks there is good news: they struggled and won.
- Send the table and your notes; every number points at a moment to tune.

### Added 2026-10-13 (first-look pass)
- **The hatch:** the pet now sits in half of its egg shell. Does the child
  connect the egg they tapped with the pet ("it came out!")?
- **My garden:** a new child's garden starts with the egg shell; tapping it
  makes the pet climb back in. Do they find the shell in the tray and try it?
- **The sleepy sky:** the letter-star tab is full of sleeping stars that peek
  when tapped. Curious, or does a sky with no letters yet feel like "nothing"?
- **The dozing pet:** leave the map alone for 40s. When the child comes back
  to a sleeping pet, delight or worry? Do they try to wake it?

### Added 2026-10-16 (coming-back pass)
- **Daily treasure:** does the child spot the glinting find on the map by
  themselves? On the next day, do they look for it ("what's here today?")?
- **Treasure shelf:** do they visit it and show it off? Does the dashed
  "more to find" cubby feel exciting, or like something missing?
- **Practice star:** do they notice the star rising from the flowers after
  a toy, and connect it to the wardrobe or stickers?
- **Growing up:** when the pet grows, watch their face. Proud, surprised,
  or unsure the pet is "the same" one?
- **Daily visitor:** does the child tap the letter friend by the stop? Do
  they say its name back after hearing it?
- **Golden treasures** (after all 16): do gold finds feel special, or just
  "more of the same"?
- **Tomorrow's bud:** does the child ask about the sleeping bud, or come back
  the next day wanting to see what it became?
